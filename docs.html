MEME MATTER

Every meme is made of the same dust The chain just rearranges it

01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Meme Matter

Twenty thousand green particles on one screen. Left alone they settle into the face of Doge. Scroll and they scatter, then gather again into Pepe, then Wojak, then Trollface, and at the end into the ticker. Move your cursor through them and they get out of the way, then flow back.

That is the whole idea. Nothing on this chain is new material, it is the same dust in a new shape.

THE DUST
One particle system, lime and cream, drawn with WebGL, no library
THE SHAPES
Four legendary faces and the ticker, in that order, one per screen
THE FORMAT
Scroll picks the shape, the mouse disturbs it, physics does the rest
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · THE SHAPESWhat the dust becomes

five screens ├── Doge the first shape, the dust likes it ├── Pepe green dust into a green frog, the easiest move ├── Wojak the dust looks sad in this one, it is the same dust ├── Troll a few particles turn pink here, nobody knows why └── the ticker the last shape, the dust holds it until you scroll back

SCROLL

05 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The particles live in your graphics card for as long as the tab is open.

MUSIC
One CC0 piano loop from OpenGameArt, plays after your first tap
PHONES
Fewer particles on small screens so it stays smooth
